# FISCAL PULSE: National Budget & Macroeconomic Simulator

**Fiscal Pulse** is an interactive, multiplayer public finance and macroeconomic simulation game designed for university students. 

Manage state budgets, navigate Keynesian multipliers, avoid Laffer curve pitfalls, and steer real historical country economies across 5 fiscal years.

## 🚀 Play Online (GitHub Pages)
Play directly in your browser without any installation:
**`https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`**

## 🏛️ Real-World Country Scenarios
1. **Singapore (1965–1970)** — *The Resource-Poor Start-up*
2. **Norway (1975–1980)** — *Managing the Oil Boom*
3. **Greece (2009–2014)** — *Sovereign Debt Crisis*
4. **Post-WWII Germany (1948–1953)** — *Wirtschaftswunder Reconstruction*

## 🎮 Features
- **Classroom Multiplayer:** Host/Student mode with live real-time cohort leaderboard synchronization via `BroadcastChannel` and `localStorage`.
- **Editorial Swiss Grid Design:** Minimalist data visualization inspired by *The Economist* and *Financial Times*.
- **Rigorous Economic Engine:** Deterministic calculations including Laffer curve yields, Keynesian multipliers, Okun's law, Solow-Romer endogenous growth lags, and sovereign debt risk premiums.
- **Historical Crisis Events:** 20 distinct historical crises across 4 countries with choices and economic cause-and-effect debriefs.
